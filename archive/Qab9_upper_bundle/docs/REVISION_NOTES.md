# Revision notes from Qab8 to Qab9

## Mathematical integration

The revised note retains the corrections made after the detailed audit of Qab7/Qab8 and adds a new Section 18 devoted to the explicit upper-unit-range elimination.  The principal additions are:

- an exact plastic-number identity proving `theta_0^70 > 2*175394637` without floating point;
- the uniform bounds `d>D/140` and `r,s<=139`;
- the full Kummer degree identity `d=r*s*k` in the unit range;
- a one-package support estimate giving `r*rad(A*B*(A+B))<4398937` and its symmetric counterpart;
- the full-radical congruence and role-binomial degree filters;
- the same-shape correspondence bound `d<=2*r*s`;
- an exact computer-assisted theorem eliminating `6816242<=D<=175394637`.

The abstract, summary theorem, residual-class discussion, computational section, and conclusion have been updated accordingly.  The note explicitly states that the remaining lower range is not yet resolved.

## Computational simplification

The proof pipeline now uses only exact integer/rational decisions.  It does not require primitive irreducibility tests, large polynomial construction, or finite-field factorization.  The exhaustive upper computation proceeds through

The 761-row file is independently checked by `code/verify_upper.py`.

## Source-audit correction

During the final code audit, the support-index optimization in `pair_upper.cpp` was found to miss a possible large prime in a scale of the form `2^a*3^b*l`: the original helper inspected the unstripped scale rather than first removing every factor of 2 and 3.  The implementation now strips those powers before extracting primes at least 5, and `--self-test-support` checks every scale through 139 against direct factorization.

This correction changes several intermediate pair counts, all of which are updated in the note and documentation.  It does **not** change the final 761-row pre-Bézout file or the zero-survivor conclusion; byte-for-byte rerun checks are recorded in `data/reproducibility_checks.txt`.

## Scope and trust boundary

The package proves the upper unit-range elimination conditional on review of the new mathematical propositions and on source-level coverage of the concise C++ enumeration.  It does not claim a proof of the full conjecture.  A future Lean-oriented version should replace the remaining source-level coverage trust with interval or branch certificates checked by a small verifier.
